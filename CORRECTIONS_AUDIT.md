7. **Theorem 1.7 (Majorant positivity & growth):** Replaced broken $|y|>2\Delta\log\Delta$ argument with exact $I_0+R_0=0$ $1/y$-cancellation on $|y|\ge 1$, and proved $\sup_x((1+x^2)|M_{\theta,\Delta}(x)|) = O_\theta(\log\Delta)$ directly from the series on $|y|<1$.
